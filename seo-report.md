## Top 3 Priority Fixes

1. **Add `<link rel="canonical">` to `privacy.html` and `terms.html`.** Both are real, indexable, footer-linked legal pages with no canonical tag — the only two indexable content pages missing one.
2. **Add JSON-LD schema markup to `contact.html`, `privacy.html`, and `terms.html`.** These are the only three indexable pages in the audit set with no `<script type="application/ld+json">` block.
3. **Expand short meta descriptions to the 120–160 char target** on `terms.html` (94), `auth.html` (97), `contact.html` (100), and `privacy.html` (118 — just 2 short). Also trim `marketplace.html` (163, 3 over).

*Note: `auth.html`, `success.html`, `cancel.html`, and `reset-password.html` intentionally carry `<meta name="robots" content="noindex, nofollow">` — they're transactional/auth flow pages, not marketing content, so this is correct practice, not a defect. Their missing/partial OG tags, canonical links, and schema are scored as low-priority ⚠️ rather than ❌ for that reason.*

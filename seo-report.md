## Summary

- **Pages audited:** 15
- **Total issues found:** 24 (7 ❌ fail, 17 ⚠️ needs improvement)
- **Top 3 priority fixes:**
  1. **Missing canonical tags on 6 pages** (`auth.html`, `privacy.html`, `terms.html`, `success.html`, `cancel.html`, `reset-password.html`) — no `<link rel="canonical">` at all, which risks duplicate-content signals and is an easy, low-risk fix.
  2. **Missing OG title/description/url on 3 pages** (`success.html`, `cancel.html`, `reset-password.html`) — these only have `og:image`; social/link-preview cards on these URLs will render incomplete.
  3. **`marketplace.html` meta description is over length** (163 chars, 3 over the 160 cap) and several pages sit well under the 120 floor (`auth.html` 97, `contact.html` 100, `privacy.html` 118, `terms.html` 94, `success.html` 48, `cancel.html` 53, `reset-password.html` 34) — tighten/expand copy to land in the 120–160 range for better SERP snippet control.

Note on check #9 (`robots: noindex`): `auth.html`, `success.html`, `cancel.html`, and `reset-password.html` all carry `<meta name="robots" content="noindex, nofollow">`. This is flagged per the audit spec since these files are in the production page list, but it may be intentional — these are transactional/auth pages (login, post-Stripe success/cancel, password reset) that arguably shouldn't rank in search. Flagging for confirmation rather than as an unambiguous defect.

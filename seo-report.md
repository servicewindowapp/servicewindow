## Top 3 Priority Fixes

1. **Add `<link rel="canonical">` to `privacy.html` and `terms.html`** — Both are indexable production pages serving real traffic; missing canonicals risk duplicate-content dilution if crawled via query strings or alternate paths.
2. **Expand meta descriptions on `contact.html` (98 chars) and `terms.html` (92 chars)** — Google truncates short snippets and is more likely to substitute its own, reducing click-through control on indexable pages.
3. **Add structured data (LocalBusiness or ContactPage schema) to `contact.html`** — It's a high-intent page and the only indexable page with full OG coverage but zero schema markup.

> **Note:** `auth.html`, `success.html`, `cancel.html`, and `reset-password.html` carry `noindex` and are utility/transactional pages. Their missing tags (canonical, OG, schema) are low priority — noindex already removes them from search. They are flagged for completeness only.
